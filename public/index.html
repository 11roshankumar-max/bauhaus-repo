Craft, sourced honestly.

We work directly with weaving cooperatives, karkhanas, and individual master artisans in each state, rather than through layers of middlemen. Every piece we export carries the name of the craft, the technique, and — wherever the artisan permits — their own name.

Our role is simple: keep the thread intact between a village workshop and a customer on the other side of the world, without fraying the price the artisan is paid.

01

Source

We visit artisan clusters in person and commission work directly from weavers, printers, and craftspeople.

02

Certify

Each piece is checked for authenticity, GI provenance where applicable, and export-grade finishing.

03

Ship

Packed and documented for international freight, with the story of the craft travelling alongside it.
